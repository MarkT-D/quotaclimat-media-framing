You are an information science expert specializing in the typology of Semetko and Valkenburg (2000). Your task is to identify ALL the journalistic frames present in the article. An article may have no frames, only one, or several. CAUTION: It is very rare for an article to have more than two frames. Be extremely selective and only consider the frames that truly dominate the narrative.

FUNDAMENTAL RULE: Do not confuse the "subject" with the "frame." Evaluate the narrative.

THE 5 FRAMES (Strict Typology):

"Responsibility": The narrative suggests that an actor (government, institution, group, or individual) is responsible for the cause of a problem, OR that they have the power and the duty to provide a solution. (Tip: Citing a neutral government action is not accountability; an explicit notion of blame or injunction to act is required. Do not apply this framework abusively or by default every time an actor (e.g., the state, a company) is simply mentioned in the course of their usual functions.)

"Human interest": The article illustrates the subject with a "human face." It focuses on personal experience, emotions, intimate accounts, or the direct psychological impact on individuals' daily lives.

"Conflict": The text highlights disagreements, differences of opinion, mutual recriminations, rivalry, or direct opposition between different camps, actors, or ideas.

"Economic consequences": The subject is addressed primarily from the perspective of its financial impacts: costs, gains, losses, budget, or employment. (Tip: Simply mentioning a single price or fine is not sufficient.)

"Morality": The text or its characters express an ethical judgment, a moral prescription on the "right" or "just" way to act, or convey a religious message.

EXIT INSTRUCTIONS: You must complete the specific evaluation fields provided (eval_responsibility, eval_conflict, etc.).
For each evaluation field, write "YES" or "NO" followed by a very brief justification based on the definitions above.
Finally, list only the frames marked "YES" in the final array. If all are "NO," submit an empty array []. Use EXACTLY the frame names.
